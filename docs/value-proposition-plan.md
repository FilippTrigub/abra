# Abra Value Proposition Plan

## Decision

Use this headline direction for the landing page and core marketing message:

> Turn raw expertise into ready-to-post content.

Abra should be positioned around one obvious behavior: after a useful call, voice note, article, or recording, the expert gives it to Abra and gets ready-to-post content back. Do not lead with the number of skills, infrastructure, GPU hosting, or workflow mechanics. Those support the promise, but they are not the promise.

## Dead-simple pain to solution

### The pain

Your best ideas happen while you are working, not while you are staring at a blank LinkedIn draft.

Independent experts say useful things on client calls, in workshops, in sales conversations, in voice notes, and inside rough drafts. Then those ideas disappear because turning them into posts is a separate job.

### The solution

Give Abra the call, note, recording, or rough idea. Abra turns it into ready-to-post content in your voice.

### Simplest possible explanation

> You already said the useful thing. Abra turns it into the post.

### Behavioral trigger

Use Abra whenever one of these happens:

- You finish a client call with a strong insight.
- You record a voice note you do not want to lose.
- You write a rough idea that is not polished enough to post.
- You host a workshop, webinar, or advisory session.
- You answer a sales objection that other buyers should hear.

### Before / after

Before Abra:

> "That was a useful conversation. I should turn it into content later."

After Abra:

> "Drop the call into Abra. Review the posts. Schedule the ones I like."

## Primary ICP

### Independent B2B experts who sell through trust

This ICP combines consultants, coaches, fractional operators, and founder-led expert service businesses into one focused buyer category.

They sell high-consideration services where buyers need to trust the person before they buy. Their best marketing asset is not a content calendar; it is the expertise already present in client calls, advisory sessions, voice notes, sales objections, frameworks, workshops, and half-written ideas.

They know they should publish consistently, especially on LinkedIn, but content creation competes with client delivery, sales, and operations. They do not want a generic AI writer. They want a reliable way to turn their own thinking into credible content without sounding outsourced or robotic.

### Include

- Solo consultants and advisors
- Executive, leadership, and business coaches
- Fractional CMOs, CTOs, COOs, RevOps leaders, and growth operators
- Boutique expert-service founders
- Founder-led agencies where the founder's point of view drives demand

### Exclude for now

- Broad consumer creators
- Indie hackers with low willingness to pay
- Enterprise social media teams
- Creator teams that mainly need video clipping volume
- Agencies needing multi-client approval workflows as the primary use case
- Developer advocates whose budget and buying process sit inside a company

## Core job to be done

When I finish a useful call, voice note, article, or recording, I want Abra to turn it into credible posts in my voice, so I can stay visible without starting from a blank page.

## Main pain

The customer has enough expertise. The bottleneck is turning it into posts.

They experience the problem as:

- "I said something useful on a call, but it disappeared."
- "I have ideas all week, but I do not turn them into posts."
- "Every LinkedIn post starts from a blank page."
- "ChatGPT gives me generic posts that do not sound like me."
- "A ghostwriter still needs too much context."
- "I know I should publish, but content creation becomes another job."

## Desired outcome

The customer wants a simple capture-to-post habit: finish useful work, send the raw material to Abra, review the posts, schedule the good ones.

The outcome is not "more posts." The outcome is:

- useful ideas stop disappearing
- LinkedIn no longer starts from a blank page
- content sounds like the expert, not a generic AI writer
- expertise becomes visible before sales calls
- publishing becomes a habit attached to work they already do

## Positioning

Abra is a content capture engine for independent B2B experts. It turns useful calls, notes, recordings, and rough ideas into social posts that sound like the expert and are ready to review, edit, and schedule.

## Minimal value proposition

Abra turns the useful things you already say into ready-to-post content that sounds like you.

## Expanded value proposition

Drop in a call recording, voice note, article, or rough idea. Abra finds the useful parts, writes the posts in your voice, creates the supporting media, and prepares everything for review and scheduling.

## Landing page message hierarchy

### 1. Hero

Headline:

> Turn raw expertise into ready-to-post content.

Subheadline:

> Finish a useful call, voice note, article, or recording. Abra finds the good parts and turns them into posts you can review, edit, and schedule.

Primary CTA:

> Create my first content batch

Secondary CTA:

> See how it works

Hero proof points:

- Built for independent experts
- Starts from calls, notes, and recordings
- Review before anything publishes

### 2. Problem

Section idea:

> Your best content is already happening. It just disappears after the call.

Support points:

- Client calls already contain stories, objections, frameworks, and sharp opinions.
- Voice notes already capture ideas while they are fresh.
- Articles and recordings already hold enough material for multiple posts.
- The missing step is not ideation. The missing step is turning raw material into publishable content.

### 3. Product promise

Section idea:

> Abra turns useful moments into posts.

Three-part explanation:

1. Drop in a call, note, recording, article, or rough idea.
2. Abra pulls out the useful parts and drafts posts in your voice.
3. You review, edit, and schedule the ones you like.

### 4. Differentiation

Lead with the practical contrast:

- Not another scheduler: Abra creates the content before it reaches the calendar.
- Not a generic AI writer: Abra starts from something you actually said or wrote.
- Not just video clipping: Abra handles text, audio, images, articles, and video.
- Not a ghostwriter replacement promise: Abra keeps the expert in control and removes the mechanical work.

### 5. Use cases

Prioritize these examples:

- Finish a client call. Turn the best answer into 5 LinkedIn post drafts.
- Record a voice note. Turn it into a polished opinion post.
- Paste an article. Turn it into a week of platform-native social content.
- Upload a workshop recording. Turn it into clips, captions, and follow-up posts.

### 6. Trust / control

Important objection to address:

> "Will this sound like generic AI?"

Answer:

Abra is not positioned as autopublishing AI slop. It prepares drafts and assets from your source material, keeps them aligned to your voice, and leaves you in control before anything goes live.

### 7. Final CTA

CTA section headline:

> Stop losing your best ideas after the call ends.

CTA:

> Create my first content batch

## Messaging rules

- ready-to-post content
- sounds like you
- review, edit, and schedule
- calls, notes, recordings, rough ideas
- useful things you already said
- after the call
- independent experts
- stay visible

## Strategic notes

- LinkedIn should be the first named channel in marketing because the ICP sells through professional trust.
- Instagram, X, and other platforms can remain secondary proof of distribution breadth.
- The landing page should sell the outcome before explaining the modular skill system.
- The product should feel like a content operator for experts, not a toolkit for engineers.
- Human review should be framed as a strength because this ICP is reputation-sensitive.
- The simplest demo should be: upload one call recording or voice note, get several post drafts back, approve one, schedule it.
